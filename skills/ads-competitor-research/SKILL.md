# Competitor research

Workflow: [workflows/competitor-analysis.md](../../workflows/competitor-analysis.md).

## Sources (legal, public)

| Source | Access | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Ad Library | official MCP `ads_library_search` (by keyword / Page); or the public site | active ads on Facebook/Instagram, start dates, formats; spend/reach only for political/social-issue ads (and EU) |
| Google Ads Transparency Center | public site (adstransparency.google.com), read in the browser | advertiser's Search/Display/YouTube ads by region |
| Competitor websites/landing pages | browser | offers, proof, forms |

Do not scrape behind logins, bypass rate limits, or use paid scraping services the user has not approved.

## What to extract

Per competitor: number of active ads, oldest still-running ad (longevity ≈ likely winner - HEURISTIC, not proof), formats (static/video/carousel, UGC vs polished), hooks, offers (free consult, % off, financing), proof (reviews, years, awards), CTAs, landing page type, geographic signals, seasonality.

## Turn it into action

- Themes table: what most competitors say (table stakes) vs what nobody says (differentiation space).
- 3-5 test ideas for the client, each as a creative brief with a hypothesis, in the client's own voice and offer.
- **Never copy competitor ads verbatim** or imitate their branding. Inspiration only.
- Label claims: "running 140+ days" is observed; "this is their best ad" is inference.
