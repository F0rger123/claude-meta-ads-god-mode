# Workflow: campaign planning

Trigger: "plan a campaign", the first half of `/ads:launch`. Skill: [ads-strategy](../skills/ads-strategy/SKILL.md).

1. **Goal + economics** - from the profile: lead value, target CPL/CPA/ROAS, monthly budget. Restate the goal as a number ("12-15 qualified kitchen leads/month at ≤$90 CPL").
2. **Offer** - what exactly is offered, why now, proof available. Weak offer = say so; it is the biggest lever.
3. **Audience + geo** - target customer, service area, exclusions, `geographic_limits`.
4. **Channel choice** - Meta, Google Search, or both, with the roles from the strategy skill and the budget reality.
5. **Architecture** - campaigns/ad sets/ad groups, naming ([naming conventions](../references/naming-conventions.md)).
6. **Measurement** - conversion event / primary actions, tracking readiness, UTMs, CRM feedback, read dates.
7. **Budget + bidding** - per platform, with expected volume ranges and learning implications.
8. **Creative + copy requirements** - number of ads, formats, briefs needed.
9. **Risks** - tracking gaps, learning limited, small audience, policy categories, seasonality, landing page issues.
10. Fill [templates/campaign-brief.md](../templates/campaign-brief.md) and get the user's OK on the brief before building.
