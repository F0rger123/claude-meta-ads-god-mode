## Bundled companion plugins (text only)

`bundles/` redistributes text-only copies (skills, agents, references; no code) of these MIT plugins, pinned to the commits in `community/sources.json`, each with its original `LICENSE` and a `SOURCE.json`. They are installed as dependencies of `ads` (Claude Code) or from `.agents/plugins/marketplace.json` (Codex). Plugin manifests in `bundles/*/.claude-plugin` and `.codex-plugin` were generated by `scripts/sync_community.py`, keeping the upstream name, author, license and version. One plugin was renamed: `claude-ads` became `agrici-ads`, because Claude Code reserves the `claude-` prefix.

| Bundle | Upstream | License |
|---|---|---|
| agrici-ads | [AgriciDaniel/claude-ads](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-ads) | MIT |
| marketing-skills | [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) | MIT |
| meta-ads-genius | [aaronpdoherty/meta-ads-genius](https://github.com/aaronpdoherty/meta-ads-genius) | MIT |

The optional `adplane` entry ([adplane/claude-plugin](https://github.com/adplane/claude-plugin), MIT plugin, hosted service terms) is fetched from its own repository at a pinned commit and is not redistributed.
